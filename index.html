17 October · A late little letter

Dear Tamanna,

First of all, I'm really sorry for wishing you so late. Your birthday was on 17 October, and I know I've missed the day. But I didn't want to miss the chance to wish you properly.

There's something I wanted to tell you. I really like talking to you. I don't even know how to explain it perfectly, but you give me a different kind of vibe. Our conversations feel special to me, and they always leave me smiling.

I don't know what the future holds for us, and I don't want to rush anything. I just hope Allah writes whatever is best for both of us. If we're meant to be part of each other's lives, may He make that journey beautiful and easy. Ameen.

Until then, I simply hope life is kind to you. May Allah bless you with good health, peace, happiness, and everything your heart is praying for.

Happy belated birthday, Tamanna. The date may have passed, but this wish comes straight from my heart. 🤍
